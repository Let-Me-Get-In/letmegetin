<objective>
Project our keys into AccesLibre's vocabulary for the feed, with the identifier resolved slug-first
(D-05), and emit the reviewed mapping document naming every place/criterion we cannot express.

Purpose: PART-01 requires the feed to be keyed on AccesLibre's identifiers in their vocabulary. The
destination threading and licence gate already exist (15-01); this plan adds the projection layer and
the mapping document. The identifier is slug-first (`erp_slug or origin_id`, D-05), consuming the
15-02 probe's recorded result — with `origin_id` as the fallback until the probe populates a slug
source (a deviation recorded explicitly, per research correction 3).

Output: a nullable `erp_slug` column, `lov2_projection.py`, `acceslibre_mapping.csv` + its loader +
prose, and the functional/unit tests for the projection and the mapping document.
</objective>
