<objective>
Make contributions shippable to a lov2 destination: add the `dual` licence (D-01/D-02), and — once a
contributor-terms decision allows it — re-stamp existing `user` contributions from `odbl` to `dual`.

Purpose: D-01's premise is that the lov2 feed would otherwise contain zero contributions, because
today `user → odbl` and `odbl × lov2_partner = false`. The CSV rows flip NEW writes to `dual`; the
re-stamp migration flips EXISTING rows, which is the irreversible, contributor-terms-gated step
(Pitfall 3). This is the last plan by design: it re-stamps existing rows and needs a human decision.

Output: the `dual` rows across the three licence CSVs, `licences sync`/`audit`, the re-stamp
migration `0015`, and a functional test that a `dual` row reaches `lov2_partner` and the migration
moves only `user` rows.
</objective>
