    - `GET /export/latest.json` -> JSONResponse of settings.export_dir / "latest.json" (json.loads), 404

      or "\\", or not ending in ".zip"; return 404 when the resolved file is missing or is not a regular
      file. Resolve with (settings.export_dir / name).resolve() and assert it starts with
      settings.export_dir.resolve() (defence in depth against traversal). No StaticFiles mount, no
      per-request generation (D-11).

    `router.include_router(export.router)` (one line each).
