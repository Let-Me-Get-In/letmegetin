seven agreed files, zip them with stdlib zipfile, expose them through `letmegetin-admin export odbl`,
write `latest.json` (snapshot + moving watermark over the MVCC boundary + retention floor), and prune the
journal and old delta ZIPs after each snapshot. Add the reviewed per-licence `attribution_text` so the
attribution renders each licence's required sentence instead of a generic "obligations" placeholder.

rendered inside it (EXP-02), built on the 14-01 journal and its `safe_seq()` boundary for the delta
watermark and the retention that keeps the journal bounded (D-15).

Output: a working `export odbl` command that writes a snapshot ZIP (+ latest.json), a moving-watermark
delta ZIP, and a `--since` cumulative catch-up ZIP (refused below the retention floor), all filtered by the
reviewed licence matrix, with retention pruning the journal and old/orphan delta ZIPs after each snapshot.
</objective>
