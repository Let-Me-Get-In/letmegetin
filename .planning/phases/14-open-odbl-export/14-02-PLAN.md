Assemble the ODbL export: stream canonical places, categories, criteria and (remapped) answers into the
seven agreed files, zip them with stdlib zipfile, expose them through `letmegetin-admin export odbl`,
write `latest.json` (snapshot + moving watermark) beside the snapshots, and prune the journal and old
delta ZIPs after each snapshot.

rendered inside it (EXP-02), built on the 14-01 journal for the moving-watermark delta and the retention
that keeps the journal bounded (D-15).

Output: a working `export odbl` command that writes a snapshot ZIP (+ latest.json), a moving-watermark
delta ZIP, and a `--since` cumulative catch-up ZIP, all filtered by the reviewed licence matrix, with
retention pruning the journal and old deltas after each snapshot.
</objective>
