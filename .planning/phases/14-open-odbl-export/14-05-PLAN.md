    The `-T` is mandatory for the systemd/SSH contexts (no TTY on a timer or over SSH).

    runs `bash scripts/export-odbl.sh <mode>` with a PATH shimmed to a FAKE `docker` (a shell shim that
    records its argv to a temp file) and a FAKE `jq` (echoes a fixed seq); assert BOTH modes (F7 — the
