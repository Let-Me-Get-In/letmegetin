<objective>
Author the five policy artefacts and the terms-of-service consent flag OSM requires, and complete
the content-aware gate and the `.osmChange` generator so it refuses to write unless every artefact
and the flag are present AND the consent is real (a human flips `pending` to `accepted`) AND the wiki
and discussion URLs are real `https://` URLs (fail closed), and it renders the per-changeset comment
that names the project and links the wiki — all without any upload.

Purpose: this is the "gate" expansion and the second half of OSM-02. It turns the publish tracer's
minimal gate and single-element serialiser into the real deliverable an operator hands to the OSM
community: the policy text is written, the consent is a human action never fabricated by the agent,
and the generator is safe by construction (no artefact, no output; no consent, no output; no network,
ever).

Output: `data/referential/osm/` (six reviewed files, with consent committed `pending`) and a
completed `osm publish` that writes `answers.osmChange` + `changeset_comment.txt` only when the
content-aware gate passes.
</objective>
