<objective>
Author the five policy artefacts and the reviewed terms-of-service flag OSM requires, and complete
the `.osmChange` generator so it refuses to write unless every artefact and the flag are present
(fail closed), emits a deterministic, correctly-versioned `<modify>` per OSM node we can key, and
renders the per-changeset comment that names the project and links the wiki — all without any upload.

gate and single-element serialiser into the real deliverable an operator hands to the OSM community:
the policy text is written, the consent flag is reviewed, and the generator is safe by construction
(no artefact, no output; no network, ever).

Output: `data/referential/osm/` (six reviewed files) and a completed `osm publish` that writes
`answers.osmChange` + `changeset_comment.txt` only when the gate passes.
