<objective>
Prove the OpenStreetMap architecture end to end on one path: a synthetic PBF streams through
`pyosmium`, becomes one `source='osm'` place row (with its OSM object version), and flows back out
as one `.osmChange` `<modify>` element — the thin vertical slice every later plan expands.

Purpose: this is the tracer. It wires the one new dependency (`pyosmium`, D-01) behind an
infrastructure adapter, mirrors the AccesLibre importer contract (streaming rows → idempotent
upsert on `(source, source_id)` → dedup-ready place), and stands up the pure `.osmChange`
serialiser, so an architectural dead-end in the C++ binding or the write-back shape is caught
after one slice instead of after the CSV completion and the policy gate are built on top of it.

Output: a pinned `pyosmium` dependency, a PBF reader that yields `OsmRow`s, a minimal
`import osm` use case + CLI writing real `place`/`place_accessibility` rows, an `osm_version`
column, and a minimal `osm publish` + `.osmChange` serialiser proven by one functional
round-trip test.
</objective>
