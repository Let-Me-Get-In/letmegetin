<objective>
Prove the OpenStreetMap architecture end to end on one path: a synthetic PBF streams through
`osmium`, becomes one `source='osm'` place row carrying the OSM object version and its full raw
tag set. This is the import half of the tracer (the publish half is 16-04).

Purpose: this is the tracer. It wires the one new dependency (`osmium`, D-01) behind an
infrastructure adapter, mirrors the AccesLibre importer contract (streaming rows → idempotent
upsert on `(source, source_id)`), and persists `osm_version` + `osm_tags` — the two facts the
`.osmChange` generator (16-04/16-05) later needs to emit a non-destructive `<modify>` (BLOCKER 1).
An architectural dead-end in the C++ binding is caught after one slice instead of after the CSV
completion and the publish gate are built on top of it.

Output: a pinned `osmium` dependency, a PBF reader that yields `OsmRow`s, an `osm_version` +
`osm_tags` migration, and a minimal `import osm` use case + CLI writing real `place` rows proven by
one functional round-trip test.
</objective>

    skip relations in this slice (no POI accessibility, out of scope). Do NOT materialise the file:
    one OsmRow is yielded at a time, and never `list(...)` inside the reader (the generator contract is
