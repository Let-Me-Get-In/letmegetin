    FastAPI). Define DocumentationLevel = Literal["none","sketch","partial","good","very_good","complete"];
    DOCUMENTATION_LEVELS (the six slugs in order); DOCUMENTATION_LADDER (a tuple of
    (slug, min_rate, max_rate) with bounds none (0.0,0.0), sketch (0.01,0.25), partial
    (0.26,0.50), good (0.51,0.75), very_good (0.76,0.99), complete (1.0,1.0) per D-01);
    documentation_level(rate: float) -> DocumentationLevel that returns the first band whose

    PlaceDetailOut.from_detail set documentation=DocumentationOut(rate=..., level=...) from
    detail.documentation. Do not add the search flag here (that is 13-02); leave
    PlaceSummaryOut.documentation defaulting to None.
