<objective>
Localize the **record path** to fr/en: the place-detail page, its 404, and every
`web/src/components/record/**` component plus the verify banner. This is the second of three i18n
plans — the dictionary and `t`/`tPlural`/`format` already exist from plan 11-01, and the auth/
account pages follow in 11-06. It also fixes three correctness bugs found in review: the
contribution form stored the French label as the value, the review list hard-coded fr-FR dates, and
`VerifyBanner` was about to grow a required `locale` that its render sites did not provide.

**Phase goal (ROADMAP):** "Prove the user journey in a browser and with assistive technology."

Purpose: the record is the product's core artifact; an English locale that leaves it French defeats
the whole A11Y-02 goal.

Output: a fully localized record path whose stored values stay locale-independent, added
`not-found.tsx` coverage, and updated record unit tests.
</objective>
